# Free F1 source-model audit

Research checked 10 September 2026. No free model with both verified SF-26 component accuracy and an explicit adaptation/web-distribution license was established. The user subsequently approved Qvist's generic 2026 concept; it now replaces the procedural car in the website.

Latest constraint: use an open license without contacting creators. The user explicitly approved Qvist's 2026 concept, completed sign-in, and supplied its complete GLB. It is integrated under CC BY 4.0 as 16 illustrative groups. Excalibur is excluded from the active route; the contact draft below is historical and must not be sent. See [current integration details](../QVIST-MODEL.md).

## Open alternative: Qvist_designs' 2026 concept

https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f1-2026-concept-polygon-model-ea3bde709b1e4dc9b0ec8557d106ed42

The public Sketchfab API confirms `isDownloadable: true` and CC BY 4.0. Its author describes it as a concept made in CAD based on the 2026 regulations. The downloadable presentation is a polygon model (1,164,174 triangles / 581,898 vertices), not verified Ferrari assembly CAD. It is an open starting point for a custom reconstruction, with attribution and change notices required and no separate creator-contact requirement. Browser performance optimization is likely needed. The original asset's individual assemblies have not been inspected or downloaded.

The Ferrari SF25 and Nimaxo concepts below were also rechecked: both remain downloadable under CC BY 4.0. An additional Ferrari 2026 concept by Flamestroke was excluded because it uses CC BY-ND (NoDerivatives).

## Ferrari SF-26 by Excalibur: closest free subject match

- Creator release: https://www.grandprixgames.org/read.php?4,1181302
- Creator-linked free archive: https://www.mediafire.com/file/7qlooti51voxutr/Ferrari_SF26_Excalibur.7z/file
- Public preview: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f1-2026-ferrari-sf-26-9d375a5bb71e4439b2637cc5e1dd9930
- About 6 MB compressed; archive inspected locally in `artifacts/free-model-audit/`.
- Includes two car GP4 files, a cockpit GP4 file credited to Fongu, TEX textures, collision data, positions, and a one-page PDF readme. Melbourne GP specification; a separate Monza update is linked by the creator.
- The readme's Copyright / Permissions section permits unchanged use in mods/leagues with credit. Using the files as a base or modifying them requires permission. Selling the files is prohibited. The intended conversion, component separation and public web delivery are not treated as authorized by this grant.
- No conversion or redistribution has been performed. The archive remains outside `dist/`. Mesh hierarchy and full internal component coverage are unverified.
- Sketchfab metadata reports `isDownloadable: false` with no license; the free download is the distinct archive explicitly linked by its creator, not extracted viewer data.
